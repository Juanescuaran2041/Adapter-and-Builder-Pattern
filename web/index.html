Applied patterns

Abstraction: Crop → PotatoCrop, QuinoaCrop, FavaBeanCrop.

Implementor: IrrigationStrategy → DripIrrigation, SprinklerIrrigation, FurrowIrrigation.

Both hierarchies grow independently: 3 crops × 3 methods without creating 9 classes. Change the method of any parcel and the bridge is reconfigured at runtime (setIrrigationStrategy).

Target: SoilMoistureSensor.readSoilPercentage().

  • ModbusSensorAdapter: raw 0–1023 register counts with a factory calibration bias.
  • LegacySerialAdapter: RS-232 text frames with checksum; corrupted frames are discarded.
  • TensiometerAdapter: soil water tension in centibars (inverted scale).

  • Frost: at ≤ 1 °C sprinklers run as frost protection; drip and furrow irrigation are suspended.
  • Growth stage: flowering needs more water, maturation needs less (avoids rot).
  • Rationed water: the reservoir is only refilled during the communal turn; if there is not enough water, irrigation is denied.
  • Efficiency: drip 90 %, sprinkler 75 %, furrow 55 % of the water reaches the roots.
